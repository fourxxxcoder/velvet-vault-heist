**Velvet Vault** — a top-down stealth heist game. One thief, one night, one diamond: slip through the Meridian Gallery past three patrolling guards and three sweeping CCTV cameras, lift the Aurora Star from the vault, and walk out the way you came in.

- Guard vision cones you have to read before you move; detection that fills, suspicions that hunt, chases that catch.
- Cameras that feed the alarm meter; a full meter locks the museum down.
- Three tools at a cost: coin lures, smoke pellets, and an EMP that kills the cameras — loudly.
- Three routes through the gallery, six side loot pieces, and a getaway that pays for speed, spare tools, and spare lives.

Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best heist game" bounty, $50 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD/arrows to move, Shift to sneak, click (or Q) to throw a lure, E for smoke, F for EMP, P to pause.
